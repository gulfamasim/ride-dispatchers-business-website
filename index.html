About Ride Dispatchers

A team that picks up the phone like it's their own fleet.

We started in 2021 because we kept watching small and mid-size fleets lose 30–40% of their late-night rides to dropped calls. Drivers idle, riders annoyed, owners burned out.

So we built the desk we wished operators had — staffed by people from the industry, working in shifts that match the way taxi work actually happens.

Our dispatch center

A taxi dispatch call center in Pakistan, run to UK standards.

Our operations floor sits in Gulberg III, Lahore — and it runs on British clock time. Pakistan is four to five hours ahead of the UK, which means our night shift covers your evening peak while it is still daylight here. That single fact is why an outsourced desk in Lahore holds answer rates that in-house rotas struggle to match at 2am.

Every agent is trained on your dispatch platform before taking a live call — Cabica, Autocab, iCabbi, Cordic or TaxiCaller — and works under your brand, your scripts and your escalation rules. You keep the software and the customer relationship. We supply the people, the shifts and the reporting.

  • Up to 70% lower cost than a UK in-house dispatch desk
  • Neutral-accent English agents, trained on taxi terminology
  • UK GDPR-aligned handling, DPA in place before go-live
