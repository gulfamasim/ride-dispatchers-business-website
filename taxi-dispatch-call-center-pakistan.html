Why Lahore

Why fleets outsource dispatch to Pakistan

The case is not just cost. It is the fact that your hardest shift to staff is our easiest one.

Your night shift is our day shift

Pakistan Standard Time runs four hours ahead of British Summer Time and five ahead of GMT. Your 9pm Friday peak lands in the Pakistani small hours, and your genuinely hard 1am–4am window is covered by agents starting a normal morning. Alert, rested staff answer better than a UK skeleton crew on a graveyard rota.

Cost that changes the maths

A UK dispatcher costs far more than their salary once you add employer NI, pension, holiday cover, recruitment, floor space and management time. A managed desk in Lahore typically lands up to 70% below that loaded figure — and it comes with the training, QA and reporting already built in.

A deep English-speaking talent pool

English is an official language of Pakistan and the medium of instruction in higher education. Lahore has a mature BPO sector, so we hire people who already know contact-centre discipline and then train them on taxi work — rather than teaching both at once.

Headcount that flexes with demand

Race day, a snowed-out Saturday, the fortnight before Christmas — volume in this industry is spiky. We roster against your actual call curve and add float capacity for the peaks, instead of asking you to carry that headcount all year.

Compliance handled up front

You stay the controller, we act as your processor, and we sign a DPA plus an IDTA or the UK Addendum before anyone takes a live call. Passenger data stays in your platform. Detail is on our GDPR page — including our sub-processor list and breach-notification commitment.

Reporting you can act on

Weekly numbers on answer time, abandonment rate, booking conversion and repeat-rider rate — the metrics that map to revenue. Not a dashboard of call volumes that tells you nothing about whether you are winning.

Platforms

We work inside the software you already run

No migration, no data export, no "our system only". Your platform stays exactly where it is.

Cabica — the platform we recommend

When a fleet asks us which dispatch platform to run, we point at Cabica. It is a cloud taxi dispatch and booking system, which matters more for an outsourced desk than it sounds: cloud-native permissioning means we can be granted precise, named, revocable access without anyone installing anything or opening a VPN into your office.

Practically, that shortens onboarding, keeps your access control clean, and makes it trivial to add or remove an agent as your rota changes. Fleets already on Cabica are usually the fastest to go live with us.

We also work daily on Autocab, iCabbi, Cordic and TaxiCaller. If you run something else, tell us — our agents have picked up unfamiliar systems inside the standard two-week onboarding before.
